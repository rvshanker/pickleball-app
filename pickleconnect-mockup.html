🧠 The Core Idea

Instead of splitting intent across menus, one green FAB answers "I want to play" with three clear paths. The bottom nav handles browsing; the FAB handles acting.

1
Start a Game= old "Create Game" — you organize
2
I'm Available= old "Post Availability" — be found
3
Join a Court Queue= new! live queue at a court

📐 Nav Structure

Bottom nav handles four passive contexts. The FAB floats above the center gap — never competes with nav items.

🏟
CourtsMap/list of courts, availability, queues
🎮
GamesBrowse & join organized games
👥
PlayersFind & connect with local players
👤
ProfileStats, rating, history, settings
