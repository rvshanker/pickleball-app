Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

PickleConnect ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile and web app that helps pickleball players find games, courts, and other players nearby. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you create an account or use the app:

  • Account credentials — email address and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Profile information — your name, skill level, age, gender, and city
  • Location — approximate location (used to show nearby courts, players, and games). We only collect location when you use the app and never track you in the background.
  • Messages — direct messages you send to other players through the app
  • Usage data — posts you create (availability, game listings) and courts you check into

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show you nearby courts, players, and games based on your location
  • To enable messaging between players
  • To display your public profile to other players (name, skill level, city)
  • To send in-app notifications (game invites, messages)
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate the app:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and file storage. Data is stored on Firebase servers. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics to understand how people use the app. No personally identifiable information is shared.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

4. Public vs. Private Information

Your name, skill level, and city are visible to other PickleConnect users. Your email address and password are never visible to other users. You control what you share in your profile and availability posts.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and posts are permanently removed within 30 days. Messages may persist in the other user's account.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us at [email protected]. We will process your request within 30 days.

You also have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request data portability

7. Children's Privacy

PickleConnect is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including Firebase's encrypted storage and HTTPS for all data in transit. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
