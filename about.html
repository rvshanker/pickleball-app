See how it works

Is it worth heading to the courts?

Every hour gets a plain verdict, from Great to Skip it, based on wind, gusts, rain, wet courts, heat and cold. Slide through the day and it shows you the best window to play.

Fair matchups in one tap

Add your players and your courts. PickleConnect draws every game and fills every court, with new partners each round when you play rotating doubles.

Scores from the court, not a clipboard

Players open the league link and enter scores on their own phones between games. Nobody has to chase results at the end of the night.

Standings that settle the debate

The table updates the moment a score goes in, with win %, points difference and a map of who you win with, and who you don't.

Any way your group plays

Run a weekly league that builds a season of standings, or a one-day tournament where you decide whether games won or points scored crowns the winner.

Everything is free with no ads. Players don't need an account to follow their league; only the organizer signs up.

Bring your crew

Get PickleConnect on your iPhone, or open it in any browser. Scan a code to start on your phone.
