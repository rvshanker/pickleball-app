Section 01
Getting Started

PickleConnect works on web browsers (desktop and mobile) and as a native iOS app via TestFlight. Here's how to get up and running.

1
Open the App
Visit pickleconnect.live in your browser, or install from TestFlight on iOS.
2
Sign In
Tap Sign In in the top-right corner. You can sign in with Google or email. First-time users will be guided to set up a profile.
3
Set Up Your Profile
Enter your name, skill level (2.0–5.0+), city, and optional details like age and gender. This helps other players find and match with you.
4
Allow Location Access
When prompted, allow location access so the app can show nearby courts and players sorted by distance.
💡 Tip
You can browse courts and players as a guest, but you'll need to sign in to check in, post availability, create games, or message other players.

Section 03
Courts & Check-In

The Courts tab lists all registered courts sorted by distance. Tap any court to see its live detail page.

Court Detail Page

Each court page has three tabs:

👥 Players 📅 Schedule ⚙️ Controls
Checking In
1
Enter Your Name
Type your name in the check-in field. Select your skill rating and how long you plan to play.
2
Choose "Now" or "Later"
Playing Now: Shows you as currently on-site. Later: Pick an arrival time — you'll appear in the "Coming Soon" schedule.
3
Optional: Add Guests & Comment
Bringing friends? Increment the guest count. Add a comment like "Looking for 4.0+ doubles" to help others know what you're looking for.
4
Check Out
When you're done, tap "Check Out" to remove yourself. Players auto-expire when their duration runs out.
Schedule Tab

The Schedule tab shows upcoming players grouped by arrival time (e.g., "⏰ 2:30 PM — 3 players"). This helps you plan when to show up based on when others will be there.

Controls Tab

Any user can update live court conditions:

🟢
Crowd Level
Light / Busy / Packed
📋
Condition
Open / Closed / Wet
💡
Lights
ON or OFF
🏟
Courts Rotating
How many courts are currently in play
💡 Tip
The court detail page also shows live weather (temperature and wind) pulled from your court's GPS coordinates.

Section 04
Player Availability

The Players tab is where you broadcast that you're available to play, and find others who are too.

Posting Your Availability
1
Tap "I'm Available"
From the Players tab or the "I WANT TO PLAY" button on Home, tap "I'm Available".
2
Choose Your Purpose
Select why you're looking to play: Looking to Play (open to anything), Need a Partner (doubles), or Drilling (practice).
3
Select Play Types
Pick one or more: Casual, Competitive, Tournament, or Drilling.
4
Set Your Availability
Choose when you're free: Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Pick a Date, or Custom Schedule (recurring weekly days with time ranges).
5
Set Time Range
Pick your start and end time so others know your window.
6
Choose Who Can See It
Control visibility with share modes:
🌍
Everyone
All PickleConnect players can see your post.
👥
Connections
Only people you've chatted with before.
👤
Select Players
Hand-pick specific players from a list.
🏷️
My Groups
Share only with members of a group you create or manage.
ℹ️ How Matching Works
When you post, the app automatically checks if any other player's availability overlaps with yours (same day, nearby location). Both players get a push notification about the match — you don't have to search manually.
Custom Schedule (Recurring)

If you play on a regular schedule, use "Custom Schedule" to set recurring days. For example, select Mon/Wed/Fri with 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM. This stays active until your chosen end date (defaults to 1 month). Other players can see your weekly availability grid and match with you.

Section 05
Games & Invites

The Games tab lets you create structured games with a date, time, and player list — and invite others to join.

Creating a Game
1
Tap "+ New Game"
From the Games tab or the "I WANT TO PLAY" → "Start a Game" option.
2
Fill in Details
Set the play type (Casual, Competitive, etc.), date, time, court name, number of players needed, and skill range. Add an optional description.
3
Set Visibility
Choose Public (anyone can see) or Private (invite-only). Set a distance radius to limit who sees it.
4
Create & Invite Players
After creating, you'll see an invite screen showing nearby players sorted by distance and skill match. Tap to select players, then send invites.
Responding to Invites

When someone invites you to a game, you'll receive a push notification. Open the Games tab to see your invites and tap Accept or Decline. The game organizer gets notified of your response.

Game Reminders

If you've accepted a game, you'll automatically get a reminder notification ~15 minutes before the game starts.

💡 Tip
Use the filters at the top of the Games tab to narrow by play type, skill level, visibility, and distance. Toggle between "Upcoming" and "My Games" to see games you've created or joined.

Section 06
Messaging

PickleConnect has built-in direct messaging so you can coordinate with other players without sharing personal contact info.

1
Start a Chat
Tap the 💬 button next to any player's name — in the Players tab, court detail, or game detail. This opens a direct chat.
2
View Your Inbox
Tap the message icon (💬) in the top-right header to see all your conversations. Unread messages show a red badge count.
3
Get Notified
When someone messages you, you'll receive a push notification and hear a chime sound (if the app is open).
⚠️ Note
Message and notification icons in the top header are only visible to signed-in users. Guest users will see a "Sign In" button instead.

Section 07
Push Notifications

PickleConnect sends smart push notifications to keep you updated without being spammy. Every notification type can be individually toggled on or off.

Notification Types
🏓
Nearby Game
A new game was created within 25 miles of you.
👥
Player Match
Another player's availability overlaps with yours.
📨
Game Invite
Someone invited you to their game.
✅
Invite Response
A player accepted or declined your invite.
🏟️
Court Joined
Someone checked in at the same court during your time slot.
⏰
Game Reminder
Your game starts in ~15 minutes.
💬
Direct Message
New message from another player.
Managing Notifications
1
Open Notification Settings
Tap your profile avatar (top-right) → Notification Settings, or tap the bell icon → ⚙ Settings.
2
Master Toggle
Turn all push notifications on or off with one switch. When off, you won't receive any push notifications.
3
Individual Toggles
Turn specific notification types on or off. For example, keep "Game Invite" on but turn off "Player Match" if you get too many.
4
Quiet Hours
Enable quiet hours to pause non-urgent notifications overnight (e.g., 10 PM – 7 AM). Game invites and urgent court alerts still come through.
Notification Bell

The bell icon in the top-right shows your recent notifications (last 7 days, up to 30). Unread notifications show a red badge count and a green dot. Tapping the bell marks all as read.

ℹ️ How "Court Joined" Works
You only get this notification if another registered player checks in at the same court AND their time overlaps with yours. Guest users and non-overlapping check-ins don't trigger it. This avoids notification spam when unrelated people are at the same venue at different times.

Section 08
Proximity Check-In Prompt

When you're physically near a court (~800 feet / 0.15 miles), the app detects it and shows a floating banner at the top of the screen:

📍 You're near Elm Park Courts!
Want to check in and let others know you're here? → Check In / Not now

Tapping "Check In" opens the court detail page where you can complete your check-in. "Not now" dismisses the prompt for that court during the current session. The app checks your proximity every 30 seconds and only prompts for courts where you haven't already checked in.

⚠️ Requires Location
This feature requires location permissions. On iOS, make sure "When In Use" location access is granted. The prompt only appears for signed-in users.

Section 10
Testing Tips & Known Behaviors
What to Test
✓
Sign In / Sign Out Flow
Try Google sign-in and email sign-in. Verify your profile loads after signing in. Check that guest icons (bell, messages) are hidden.
✓
Court Check-In
Check in as "Now" and "Later" at a court. Verify you appear in the right section. Check out and confirm removal.
✓
Availability Post
Post with different share modes (Everyone, Connections, Select Players). Verify others can see it when expected.
✓
Create & Join a Game
Create a game, invite a player, accept/decline from the other account. Verify notifications are received.
✓
Messaging
Send a message between two accounts. Verify the notification appears and the badge count updates.
✓
Notification Toggles
Disable a notification type, trigger the event, and verify no push is received. Re-enable and confirm it works.
✓
Nearby Courts (Location)
Allow location access and verify courts are sorted by distance. On mobile, confirm the proximity check-in prompt appears when near a court.
Reporting Issues

When reporting a bug, please include:

📱 Device (iPhone 15, Chrome desktop, etc.) 🔄 Steps to reproduce 📸 Screenshot if possible ⏰ Time it happened
⚠️ Known Behaviors
Browser warning: You may see a Babel transformer warning in the console — this is expected during development.

Push notifications on iOS web: iOS Safari requires adding the app to your Home Screen (PWA mode) for web push notifications to work. The native TestFlight build handles push natively.

First-time notification permission: When you first sign in, the app will ask for notification permission. If you deny it, you can re-enable it in your browser/device settings.
