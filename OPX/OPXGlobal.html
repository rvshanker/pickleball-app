The problem we solve

Two things quietly drain every practice.

Modern healthcare runs on paperwork. The result is exhausted providers and revenue that slips through the cracks. OPX removes both.

Problem 01

Administrative burnout

Calls, faxes, scheduling, scribing, and insurance navigation pull your clinical team away from patients. The non-clinical workload never stops — and it's not what they trained for.

Problem 02

Revenue leakage

Denied claims, slow credentialing, and rising days in A/R mean money you've earned arrives late or never at all. Small inefficiencies compound into real losses every month.

OPX takes both off your plate.
