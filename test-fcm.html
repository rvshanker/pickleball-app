🗺 COMPATIBILITY GUIDE
✅ Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
Full support. Notifications arrive even when browser is in background. Works out of the box with this setup.
✅ Android (Chrome)
Full support. Add to home screen (PWA) for app-like experience with notification badges.
⚠️ iPhone / iPad Safari (iOS 16.4+)
Supported only if the site is added to Home Screen (PWA mode). Steps:
 1. Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
 2. Open the app from Home Screen (not Safari)
 3. Grant notification permission when prompted
Does NOT work in regular Safari tab or any other iOS browser.
❌ iPhone – Chrome / Firefox / other browsers
Apple restricts web push to Safari PWA only on iOS. Chrome/Firefox on iPhone cannot receive web push regardless of settings.
💡 Recommendation for PickleConnect
Prompt players to add the site to their home screen. iOS 16.4+ is on ~90% of active iPhones as of 2025. Show the "Add to Home Screen" prompt at first visit for the best mobile experience.
