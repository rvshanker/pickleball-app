Frequently Asked Questions
How does the weather rating work?
▼
Open Weather and allow location access or search for a city or ZIP code. Each hour is rated Great, Okay, Marginal or Skip it. Wind and gusts count the most, then rain (and courts that may still be wet after rain), then heat and cold. Thunderstorms are always rated Skip it. The page also shows the best 2-hour window to play each day.
How do I create a league or tournament?
▼
Open Leagues & Tournaments, sign in, and tap "+ New." Choose singles, fixed-partner doubles or rotating-partner doubles, add your players or teams and your courts, then tap "Make the schedule." Share the league link with your group so everyone can see who plays on which court.
How are standings worked out?
▼
Standings update as soon as scores are entered. Open the league and tap "How ranking works" under the standings for the exact rules your league uses.
How do I create an account?
▼
You only need an account to organize. Open Leagues & Tournaments and tap "Sign up" to create one with your email and a password. Players don't need an account: they open the link the organizer shares to see their courts, scores and standings. Weather needs no account at all.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
▼
On the Leagues log-in screen, tap "Forgot password?", enter your email and check your inbox (and spam folder) for the reset link.
How do I delete my account?
▼
Log in to Leagues & Tournaments, scroll to the bottom of your list and tap "Delete my account". Type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account and every league and tournament you created, with their scores. It can't be undone.
Is PickleConnect free?
▼
Yes! PickleConnect is completely free to use. There are no ads, no premium tiers, and no hidden costs. We built it because we love pickleball.
What devices are supported?
▼
PickleConnect works on any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on desktop and mobile. Get the iPhone app on the App Store. On Android, open pickleconnect.live in Chrome and choose "Add to Home screen."
